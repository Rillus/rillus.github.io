This is the privacy policy for **Karaoke Lyrics**, an Even Realities G2 glasses app made by Riley Ramone.

Last updated: 18 November 2026.

## What the app does

The app shows song lyrics on your glasses and can advance them as you sing.

## Microphone

The app uses the glasses microphones to follow your singing. Audio is processed on your phone in memory so the lyrics can move along. It is never stored, and it is never sent to me or to any other service.

## Network

The app uses the network for two things only:

1. **Lyrics search.** If you search for a song, the app sends your search terms to [LRCLIB](https://lrclib.net) and may download the lyrics you choose. Those results stay in memory for the current session and are not saved on the phone.
2. **Speech model.** On first use the app may download a small speech-recognition model (Vosk `vosk-model-small-en-us-0.15`, Apache-2.0) from this site (`ramone.co`) so sung words can be matched on your phone. The file is cached on the phone. Audio is not uploaded with this request.

No other sites are contacted.

## What is stored on your phone

- Songs you paste are saved in your phone's browser `localStorage` and can be deleted in the app.
- The speech model may be kept in the phone's Cache Storage so it does not need to be downloaded again.

I do not have access to this data.

## What is not collected

There is no account, no advertising and no analytics. The app does not use tracking cookies or send usage data to me.
